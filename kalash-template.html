About Chitral & Kalash Valleys

The three Kalash valleys shelter one of the smallest and oldest living cultures in South Asia, with its own language, wooden shrines and seasonal festivals, set beneath the peaks of the Hindu Kush. Chitral town, with its fort and Shahi Mosque, is the gateway.

Visitor Notes
Best time to visit

May and August for festivals; June – October generally

Time needed

3–4 days from Chitral

Good to know

This is a living community, not an exhibit: always ask before photographing people and follow local guidance.
