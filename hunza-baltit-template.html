About Hunza & Baltit Fort

Baltit Fort has watched over the Hunza valley for roughly 700 years. Its Tibetan-influenced timber and stone structure, rebuilt over centuries as the seat of the Mirs of Hunza, was restored in the 1990s — today it is a museum with terraces looking straight at Rakaposhi and Ultar Sar.
