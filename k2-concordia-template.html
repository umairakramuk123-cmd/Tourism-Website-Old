About K2 Base Camp & Concordia

Concordia is the great glacier junction where the Baltoro and Godwin‑Austen glaciers meet, ringed by four of the world’s fourteen 8,000‑metre peaks including K2 at 8,611 m. Reaching it is a full expedition: roughly two weeks on foot from Askole with porters, a licensed operator and camp support.
